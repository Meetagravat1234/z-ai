## Why Bengaluru salaries matter in 2026

Bengaluru hosts an estimated 1.5 million IT professionals — the densest tech talent pool in India. If you're a software engineer considering a move, negotiating a raise, or evaluating an offer, knowing the real numbers matters. This guide breaks down actual salary ranges by experience, company type, tech stack, and role — based on data from 300+ verified job listings on Hirebase plus industry reports from late 2025 and early 2026.

## Software engineer salary in Bengaluru by experience

The biggest driver of salary is experience level. Here's what you can realistically expect at each stage:

### 0-2 years (Entry level / SDE-I)

- **Services companies (TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Cognizant):** ₹3.5 - 7 LPA. Most freshers join at ₹3.5-4.5 LPA. After 2 years, expect ₹5-7 LPA.
- **Indian product startups (Swiggy, Zomato, Razorpay, PhonePe):** ₹8 - 15 LPA. Top performers with strong CS fundamentals and a few good projects can hit ₹18-20 LPA.
- **Global product companies (Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Meta):** ₹15 - 28 LPA base + ₹10-25 LPA in RSUs (total comp ₹25-50 LPA). These are the famous "₹40+ LPA fresher" offers you hear about.
- **Mid-tier SaaS (Freshworks, Zoho, Postman):** ₹7 - 14 LPA. Strong learning, better work-life balance than Big Tech.

### 3-5 years (Mid level / SDE-II)

- **Services companies:** ₹8 - 15 LPA. Plateau hits hard here — many engineers leave for product companies.
- **Indian product startups:** ₹15 - 30 LPA base + 5-15 LPA ESOPs.
- **Global product companies:** ₹30 - 60 LPA total comp (base + RSUs). Google L4 / Amazon SDE-II / Microsoft SDE-II levels.
- **Mid-tier SaaS:** ₹15 - 28 LPA.

### 6-10 years (Senior / SDE-III / Staff)

- **Services companies:** ₹15 - 25 LPA. Manager track (₹20-35 LPA) often pays more than IC track.
- **Indian product startups:** ₹30 - 55 LPA + significant ESOPs.
- **Global product companies:** ₹60 - 120 LPA total comp. Google L5 / Amazon SDE-III / Staff levels.
- **Mid-tier SaaS:** ₹30 - 55 LPA.

### 10+ years (Principal / Staff / Distinguished)

- **Global product companies:** ₹1 - 3 Cr total comp at L6/L7 levels.
- **Indian unicorns (post-IPO):** ₹70 LPA - ₹1.5 Cr base + ESOPs that may or may not be worth anything.
- **Services companies (Director level):** ₹40 - 80 LPA.

## Salary by tech stack

Your tech stack affects salary more than most engineers realise. As of 2026, here's what pays best in Bengaluru:

- **AI/ML engineers (Python, PyTorch, LLMs):** 30-50% premium over general backend. SDE-I with ML specialty often starts at ₹15-20 LPA vs ₹10-12 LPA for general backend.
- **Blockchain / Web3:** 20-40% premium, but fewer openings. Volatile market.
- **Backend (Java, Spring Boot):** Saturated. Average pay. Many openings but also many candidates.
- **Backend (Go, Rust):** 15-25% premium over Java. Rare skill, well-paid.
- **Frontend (React, Next.js, TypeScript):** Strong demand. 10-20% premium over Angular.
- **DevOps (Kubernetes, Terraform, AWS):** High demand, 20-30% premium over general backend.
- **Mobile (Android / iOS native):** Stable demand, average pay. Cross-platform (React Native, Flutter) pays slightly less.

## Salary by company type — the real trade-offs

Not all ₹20 LPA jobs are equal. Here's what you're really signing up for:

### Global product companies (Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Meta, Netflix)
**Pros:** Highest total comp. Strong ESOPs/RSUs. World-class engineering culture. Good WLB (Google, Microsoft). Brand value on resume.
**Cons:** Slow promotion cycles. Politics at senior levels. Stock-heavy comp (40-60% of TC is RSUs that vest over 4 years). Layoff risk in down cycles.
**Verdict:** Best for engineers who want top pay + brand. Avoid if you crave autonomy or hate process.

### Indian product startups (Swiggy, Zomato, Razorpay, PhonePe, Cred, Groww)
**Pros:** Fast promotions. Real ownership. ESOPs that may pay out if the company IPOs. Strong learning curve.
**Cons:** Lower base than Big Tech. Long hours (50-60/week common). ESOPs are paper money until liquidity event.
**Verdict:** Best for 0-5 year engineers who want rapid growth. Switch to Big Tech at SDE-II level for the pay bump.

### Mid-tier SaaS (Freshworks, Zoho, Postman, Hasura)
**Pros:** Best WLB in the industry. Stable pay. Strong engineering culture. Real products used globally.
**Cons:** Slower career growth than startups. Lower pay ceiling than Big Tech.
**Verdict:** Best for engineers who want stability + good pay + family life.

### Services companies (TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Cognizant, Accenture, Capgemini)
**Pros:** Job security. Onsite opportunities (US/Europe). Good for freshers to learn fundamentals.
**Cons:** Lowest pay. Slow promotions. Bureaucratic culture. Often bench time.
**Verdict:** Best for freshers who can't crack product companies yet. Use 2-3 years here as a springboard.

## Negotiating your salary — practical tactics

If you've gotten an offer, here's how to actually negotiate higher:

1. **Always have a competing offer.** This is the single biggest lever. Companies move 20-40% on base when they know you have a competing offer in hand.
2. **Quote total comp, not base.** Startups often have low base but high ESOPs. Compare total comp apples-to-apples.
3. **Negotiate RSU grants, not just base.** At Big Tech, RSU refreshers happen annually — pushing for a higher initial grant compounds over 4 years.
4. **Don't reveal your current salary.** Many Indian companies ask. Politely decline or quote total comp including bonuses.
5. **Ask for joining bonus.** ₹2-5 LPA joining bonus is standard at product companies. Use it to bridge a base gap.
6. **Time your switch.** Annual appraisal cycles mean April-May and October-November are when most engineers switch — companies are more desperate in Jan-March.

## Cost of living in Bengaluru — what ₹20 LPA actually buys

Salary numbers without cost-of-living context are misleading. A ₹20 LPA salary in Bengaluru:

- **Take-home pay:** ~₹1.3 LPA/month after taxes and standard deductions.
- **Rent (1BHK in Whitefield/Koramangala):** ₹20,000 - 35,000/month. 2BHK in prime areas: ₹40,000 - 60,000.
- **Groceries + food:** ₹8,000 - 15,000/month for a single person.
- **Transport (own vehicle + fuel):** ₹5,000 - 8,000/month.
- **Health insurance + family:** ₹5,000 - 12,000/month.
- **Discretionary (eating out, entertainment, subscriptions):** ₹10,000 - 25,000/month.

**Bottom line:** A ₹20 LPA engineer in Bengaluru can comfortably save ₹50,000 - 70,000/month (₹6-9 LPA/year). Compare to ₹15 LPA in Hyderabad where cost of living is 15-20% lower — same net savings.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the average salary of a software engineer in Bengaluru?

The average software engineer salary in Bengaluru is approximately ₹12-18 LPA across all experience levels. For freshers (0-2 years), the average is ₹6-10 LPA. For mid-level (3-5 years), it's ₹15-25 LPA. For senior (6-10 years), ₹30-50 LPA. Big Tech salaries skew these averages significantly upward.

### Is Bengaluru or Hyderabad better for software engineers in 2026?

Both cities offer similar salary ranges for the same role. Hyderabad has lower cost of living (15-20% cheaper rent), so net savings are often higher. Bengaluru has more startup opportunities and a denser tech community. Choose Bengaluru for career growth + networking, Hyderabad for savings + family life.

### What is the starting salary for a software engineer in Bengaluru?

Starting salary for a software engineer in Bengaluru ranges from ₹3.5 LPA (services companies like TCS, Infosys, Wipro) to ₹15-28 LPA (global product companies like Google, Microsoft, Amazon). The median for a fresher from a tier-2 college is ₹6-8 LPA. Tier-1 (IIT, NIT, BITS) freshers at product companies typically start at ₹15-25 LPA.

### How much does a software engineer with 5 years experience earn in Bengaluru?

A software engineer with 5 years experience in Bengaluru earns ₹15-30 LPA at product startups, ₹30-50 LPA at global product companies (Google L4, Amazon SDE-II), and ₹10-15 LPA at services companies. The wide range reflects the company type premium — same engineer can 3x their salary by switching from services to Big Tech.
