## Why data scientist salaries in India are surging in 2026

Data Scientist roles have seen the fastest salary growth in Indian tech over the past 3 years. With the AI/ML boom following ChatGPT's release, every product company in India is hiring data scientists — and the supply of qualified candidates hasn't kept up.

This guide breaks down actual salary numbers by city, experience, company type, and specialization — based on 50+ verified data scientist job listings on Hirebase plus data from levels.fyi, Glassdoor, and AmbitionBox.

## Data scientist salary in India by experience level

### 0-2 years (Entry level / Associate Data Scientist)

- **Services companies (TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Cognizant):** ₹5 - 9 LPA. Most freshers join here, often transitioning from software engineering roles.
- **Indian product startups (Swiggy, Zomato, Razorpay, PhonePe):** ₹10 - 18 LPA base + ESOPs.
- **Global product companies (Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Meta):** ₹18 - 30 LPA base + ₹10-25 LPA RSUs (total comp ₹28-55 LPA).
- **Mid-tier SaaS (Freshworks, Zoho, Postman):** ₹10 - 16 LPA.

### 3-5 years (Mid level / Data Scientist)

- **Services companies:** ₹10 - 18 LPA.
- **Indian product startups:** ₹18 - 35 LPA base + ESOPs.
- **Global product companies:** ₹35 - 70 LPA total comp (Google L4, Microsoft Senior DS, Amazon SDE-II equivalent).
- **Mid-tier SaaS:** ₹20 - 35 LPA.

### 6-10 years (Senior / Senior Data Scientist / Lead)

- **Services companies:** ₹18 - 30 LPA.
- **Indian product startups:** ₹35 - 65 LPA + significant ESOPs.
- **Global product companies:** ₹70 - 1.5 Cr total comp.
- **Mid-tier SaaS:** ₹40 - 60 LPA.

### 10+ years (Staff / Principal / Head of Data Science)

- **Global product companies:** ₹1.5 - 3 Cr total comp.
- **Indian unicorns (Director of Data Science):** ₹80 LPA - ₹1.5 Cr base + ESOPs.
- **Services companies (Senior Manager level):** ₹40 - 80 LPA.

## Data scientist salary by specialization

Not all "data scientist" roles pay the same. Specialization matters a lot:

### Machine Learning Engineer

- **Premium:** 20-40% above general data scientist
- **Why:** Production ML engineering is rarer than analytics. Engineers who can deploy models at scale are scarce.
- **Required skills:** Python, PyTorch/TensorFlow, MLOps, model serving (TF Serving, Triton), Kubernetes, AWS SageMaker.

### Deep Learning / NLP Engineer

- **Premium:** 30-60% above general data scientist
- **Why:** Post-LLM boom, every company wants NLP/LLM engineers. Severe supply shortage.
- **Required skills:** Transformers, Hugging Face, fine-tuning LLMs (Llama, Mistral, GPT), RAG systems, vector databases (Pinecone, Weaviate).

### Computer Vision Engineer

- **Premium:** 15-30% above general data scientist
- **Why:** CV is niche but well-established in industries like autonomous driving, medical imaging, surveillance.
- **Required skills:** OpenCV, PyTorch, YOLO, segmentation models, image processing.

### Data Analyst / Business Analyst

- **Discount:** 30-50% below data scientist
- **Why:** Lower technical bar — mostly SQL + dashboards + stakeholder management.
- **Required skills:** SQL, Excel, Python (basic), Tableau/Power BI, stakeholder communication.

### Data Engineer

- **Pay range:** Similar to data scientist (sometimes higher)
- **Why:** Strong demand — data engineers build the pipelines that data scientists depend on. Often under-supplied.
- **Required skills:** SQL, Python, Spark, Airflow, Snowflake, BigQuery, AWS/GCP data services.

## How to negotiate a higher data scientist salary

If you've gotten an offer, here's how to push for more:

1. **Get a competing offer.** This is non-negotiable. Without a competing offer, companies won't budge beyond 5-10% on base.
2. **Highlight niche skills.** LLM fine-tuning, MLOps, distributed ML — these command premiums. Quantify your experience.
3. **Showcase production impact.** "Built ML model that improved CTR by 12%" beats "Trained 5 models on Kaggle datasets".
4. **Negotiate ESOPs aggressively.** For startups, ESOPs are 20-40% of total comp. Don't just negotiate base.
5. **Time your switch.** April-May (post-appraisal season) and October-November are when most engineers switch. Jan-March is when companies are most desperate.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the salary of a data scientist in India?

The average data scientist salary in India is ₹12-25 LPA across all experience levels. Freshers earn ₹5-15 LPA. Mid-level (3-5 years) earn ₹18-45 LPA. Senior (6-10 years) earn ₹40-90 LPA. Big Tech (Google, Microsoft, Amazon) pays ₹28-55 LPA for freshers and ₹70 LPA - 1.5 Cr for senior roles.

### Which city pays data scientists the most in India?

Bengaluru pays data scientists the most in India — average ₹18-28 LPA across all experience levels, with senior roles at top product companies reaching ₹70-90 LPA. Hyderabad is a close second with 15-20% lower cost of living. Mumbai pays well for fintech/quant roles (₹13-22 LPA average). See our [jobs in Bengaluru](/jobs/bengaluru) page for current openings.

### Is data science still a good career in 2026?

Yes — data science is one of the highest-paying tech careers in 2026, especially with the AI/LLM boom. Demand for ML engineers with LLM/NLP skills has grown 3x year-over-year. However, entry-level competition has increased — many freshers with bootcamp certifications struggle. The path to high pay now requires real production experience (not just Kaggle) and specialization in LLMs, MLOps, or computer vision.

### How much does a data scientist earn in Google India?

Google India pays data scientists (Research Scientist / ML Engineer roles) ₹35-70 LPA total comp at L4 level (3-5 years experience), ₹70 LPA - 1.5 Cr at L5 (6-10 years), and ₹1.5-3 Cr at L6+ (10+ years). Total comp includes base + RSUs (40-60% of TC) + target bonus. Google's data scientist roles typically require a Master's or PhD in CS/Stats/ML.

### Do data scientists earn more than software engineers in India?

In India, data scientists typically earn 10-30% more than software engineers at the same experience level, due to scarcity of qualified candidates. However, top-tier software engineers at Big Tech (Google L5, Amazon SDE-III) earn as much as senior data scientists. The premium is largest at entry level (data scientist fresher ₹8-15 LPA vs SDE fresher ₹6-12 LPA at product companies).
